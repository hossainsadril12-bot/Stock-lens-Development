# StockLens — User Guide

StockLens keeps track of your stock. It changes shape to fit what you sell — a warehouse, real-estate units, equipment, software licences, or kits. The screens, columns, and words follow the type you pick.

This guide is split by **who you are**. Find your role and read that part.

| **Staff** | [Everyone](#part-1--everyone) + [Staff](#part-2--staff) |

# Part 1 — Everyone

Everyone can do the following, whatever your role.

## Sign in and set up

1. Sign in with the email and password you were given.
2. Pick the industries you manage. Choose one or several, then press **Continue**.
3. (Admin/Staff only) Upload a spreadsheet of stock now, or press **Skip for now**.
4. You land on the dashboard.

## Switch workspace

If you picked more than one industry, use the switcher in the top bar (next to the company name) to move between them — for example **Warehouse** to **Real Estate**. Each keeps its own items and dashboard.

## Read the dashboard

The dashboard shows your key numbers, a **Needs you today** band for things that need action, a trend chart, and recent activity.

## Everyday basics

- **Notifications:** the bell in the top bar shows updates. A red number means unread.
- **Sort a table:** click any column heading to sort. Click again to reverse.
- **Your account:** click your name (top right) for **User Guide**, **Settings**, **Privacy**, **About**, and **Sign out**.
- **Light or dark:** use the sun / moon button in the top bar.

## Send feedback

If something does not work or feels confusing, open the **User Guide** (top-right menu), scroll to the bottom, and use **Send feedback**. We read every message.

# Part 2 — Staff

Everything in Part 1, plus the daily work below.

## Items and stock

Open **Items** from the left menu.

- **Add an item:** press **Add product**, fill the form (name, category, quantity, price…), save. Fields change to match your industry.
- **Edit an item:** click its name, then **Edit**, change details, save. *(Staff cannot delete items — ask an Admin.)*
- **Add stock by scanning:** press **Scan barcode**. Use a USB scanner or type the code and press Enter. A known code adds one to stock. An unknown code offers to make a new item.
- **Import a spreadsheet:** press **Import CSV**, download the template, fill it, upload, check the preview, import.

## Sell to a customer (checkout)

1. On **Items**, press **Checkout**.
2. Scan each product, or add it from the list. Scan again for another of the same.
3. Type the customer name.
4. Press **Complete sale**. Stock drops and a receipt opens — press **Print receipt**.

*(To sell a single item you can also open it and use **Issue to customer**.)*

## Categories

Open **Categories** to add, rename, or delete groups like *Paper* or *Ink*. Click a category to open it and scan barcodes to add stock straight into that group.

## Purchase orders (buying stock)

1. Open **Purchase Orders**, press **New PO**, pick the item, quantity, supplier, expected date.
2. The PO waits for an **Admin to approve** it.
3. When the goods arrive, press **Receive**. Stock is added to the warehouse and the Admin is notified.

## Transfers (stock arriving from another warehouse)

1. Press **Request transfer** to ask for stock from another location.
2. When it arrives, press **Received**.
3. Stock updates and the Admin is notified that you received it.

## Reports

Open **Reports** to see stock movements, low stock, and stock value. You can export.

# Part 3 — Admin / Owner

Everything in Parts 1 and 2, plus full control below.

## Items — full control

Admins can do everything Staff can, and also **delete** an item: open the item, press **Delete**, and confirm.

## Suppliers

Open **Suppliers** (Admin only).

- **Add:** press **Add supplier**, enter name, phone, lead time.
- **Edit / Delete:** use the buttons on each row. Delete asks you to confirm.

## Purchase orders — approve and receive

- **Approve:** a staff-raised PO shows **Pending approval**. Press **Approve** to send it to the supplier.
- **Receive:** when goods arrive, press **Receive** — stock is added automatically.

## Transfers — dispatch and approve

- **Dispatch:** create a transfer and assign a vehicle and driver.
- **Approve:** approve or reject staff transfer requests.
- Staff at the receiving end press **Received**; you get a notification.

## Employees and drivers

Open **Employees** (Admin only).

- **Staff tab:** add people who log in. Use **Add employee**, then **Edit** or **Delete** on each row. You cannot delete yourself.
- **Drivers tab:** add drivers who carry transfers. Use **Add driver**, **Edit**, **Delete**.
- **Transport:** manage vehicles and assign drivers.

## Notifications inbox

The bell shows when staff receive purchase orders or transfers. Opening it marks messages read.

## Feedback inbox

Open the **User Guide** and scroll to **Feedback received** — every message users send appears there, with who sent it and when.
